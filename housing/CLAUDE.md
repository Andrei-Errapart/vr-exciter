# CLAUDE.md — housing/

Parametric build123d models of the closed-drum housing for the HDD exciter
(base, cover, B head, thread coupon). Read `README.md` first. It has the
parts, BOM, assembly and adjustments. The design is in
`../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-hdd-housing-extension-design.md`, the
build plan in `../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-09-hdd-housing.md`.

## Source vs. generated

- `src/housing/*.py` and `tests/` are the **source**.
  - `params.py` holds **every number**. Don't put dimensions in the other
    modules; add a named parameter with a comment on where it comes from.
  - `base.py`, `cover.py`, `b_head.py`, `joint.py`, `coupon.py` build the
    parts; `b_frame.py` has B's local-frame helpers that the head and the
    base share. `reference.py` loads the drive and board. `checks.py` and
    `analysis.py` hold the geometric, load and statics checks. `bambu3mf.py`
    writes the 3MFs, `build.py` runs everything.
- `step/*.step` and `*_P1S.3mf` are generated by `make-housing build` and are
  committed. Rebuild and commit them with the change that alters them.
- `build/` (including `assembly.step`) is generated and ignored by git.

## Commands

- `housing/make-housing test` runs pytest; `build` writes the files
  (`build --c-thread UNF_5_8_18` builds the cover for a 5/8"-18 UNF sensor
  instead of `C_THREAD`'s). Both create `.venv` first.
- **Python 3.12 through uv.** The system Python (3.15) is too new for
  build123d's OCP wheels. Don't recreate the venv with another version.
- **The board export must be fresh.** `reference.py` reads
  `../hdd-driver/build/test-model/`, made by
  `hdd-driver/scripts/export-test-model`. Rerun it after any change to the
  board; the tests refuse a stale export. The board feature positions in
  `params.py` (buttons, trimmer, J1/J3 wire heights, USB mouth) were measured
  from it and must be re-measured when the layout changes.

## Conventions

- **Drive frame:** as in `../hdd-driver/CLAUDE.md`: X right of the centre
  line, Y down from the connector end, bottom view. The spindle is at
  `SPINDLE`.
- **Z:** height above the drive's underside (Z 0), positive towards the disc.
  The base seat and the cover's mating face are at Z 0; the tray floor is
  below (`FLOOR_TOP_Z`), the cover plate above (`PLATE_Z`).
- **Polar:** `pol(r, phi, t)` gives drive-frame (x, y) about the spindle. phi
  is from +X, positive towards -Y. B is at `B_PHI`, C at `C_PHI`.
- **Units:** mm and degrees.
- **Print orientation:** the parts are modelled in the drive frame. `build.py`
  flips the cover (top face down) for printing; the base prints floor down,
  the B head upright.

## Things that are easy to get wrong

- B is adjusted from **below**. Three push set screws (`B_PUSH_POINTS`) hold
  the head up; the one pull screw (`B_PULL_POINTS`) must stay at least
  `B_SUPPORT_MARGIN` inside their triangle, or its preload tilts the head
  onto its tongue (Ruling 21). `tests/test_b_mount.py` checks this from the
  holes in the parts and by turning the head about each edge.
- Screw lengths (`B_PULL_SCREW_LEN`, `B_PUSH_SCREW_LEN`) are tied to the
  insert engagement checks in the tests. Change them together with the
  geometry, not alone.
- `KEY_ANGLE` is not 270 on purpose: the tab angles repeat under a half turn,
  so only the key stops a reversed cover.
- A slot or hole added for a cable or probe changes the safety claim in
  `README.md` (fingers can't reach the disc). Re-check the opening tests:
  in `tests/test_base.py` the USB tunnel, the 12 V, J1 and J3 slots, the poke
  holes and the floor slots; in `tests/test_cover.py` the vents. Update the
  README's list of openings.
- The committed cover has the M16×1.5 thread (`C_THREAD`). A
  `build --c-thread` run rewrites the committed cover files too.
- Burst containment is not claimed anywhere. Don't write that it is.
