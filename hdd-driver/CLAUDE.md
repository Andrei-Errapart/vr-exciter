# CLAUDE.md — hdd-driver/

KiCad 10 project for the board that drives the HDD spindle motor: a WeAct
STM32G474 Core Board and a SimpleFOC Mini v1.0 (DRV8313) on a carrier
screwed to the drive's underside. Read `README.md` first. It has the drive
geometry, pin assignment, power scheme and open checks.

## Source vs. generated

- `hdd-driver.kicad_sch` and `hdd-driver.kicad_pcb` are the **source**. They
  were generated once by scripts that no longer exist; edit them, don't
  regenerate them. Before rewriting either file, make sure the project is not
  open in KiCad. `kicad-cli` can leave a stale `~hdd-driver.kicad_pro.lck`
  behind, so a lock file alone doesn't prove the project is open: check with
  `lsof`.
- `lib/hdd-driver.kicad_sym` and `lib/hdd-driver.pretty/` came from
  `scripts/library/gen_*.py`. Rerunning those overwrites edits made in KiCad.
  Change the generator and the library together, or only the library.
- `lib/3d/*.step` are generated by `scripts/module-models/make-module-models`
  from `lib/3d/src/*.kicad_pcb`. `build/` is generated by
  `scripts/export-test-model` and is ignored by git.

## Conventions

- **Drive frame:** X to the right of the drive centre line, Y down from the
  connector end, bottom view. It equals PCB coordinates minus (150, 40) mm,
  and the board's aux/grid origin is set there. Give positions in this frame.
- **Pad numbers:**
  - WeAct pads are `P1.n` / `P2.n` (header P1/P2 pin n).
  - SimpleFOC pads 1–10 are module header H1 pins; 11 = `+`, 12 = `−`,
    13–15 = M1–M3.
  - Spring contacts are pads 1–3 (B.Cu), and pad 4 is the screw-7 hole.
  - H1–H3 are the M2 drive screws (holes 2, 3, 8); H4–H7 are the optional
    M3 corner holes. J2, H1–H3 are fixed to the drive; everything else can
    move.
  - Symbol pin numbers match these.
- **3D models** are referenced as `${KIPRJMOD}/lib/3d/...`. When running
  `kicad-cli` on a copy of the board elsewhere, pass
  `-D KIPRJMOD=<this dir>`.
- **Model frames:** module models have their origin at the module centre
  and z = 0 at the module PCB underside. Socketed module footprints raise
  them by 11.04 mm (8.5 mm socket + 2.54 mm header plastic).
- **KiCad 3D model Z rotation** turns the opposite way to footprint
  rotation (see `stock_model_on_pads` in `gen_footprints.py`).

## Tools

- **KiCad 10.0** is in `/Applications/KiCad`. Its Python
  (`.../Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/Current/bin/python3`)
  has `pcbnew`. `kicad-cli` prints German messages.
- **Checks:**
  - `kicad-cli sch erc hdd-driver.kicad_sch` should give 0 violations.
  - `kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity hdd-driver.kicad_pcb` gives only
    unconnected items until the board is routed.
- **Renders:**
  `kicad-cli pcb render --side top|bottom [--rotate 'x,y,z'] -o x.png hdd-driver.kicad_pcb`.
- **WeAct STEP stripping** needs `cadquery-ocp`, which is not installed
  globally. Use a venv.

## Hardware facts that are easy to get wrong

- **WeAct `VCC`** is the LDO input (3.3–20 V), fed from VBUS through a
  Schottky diode. VBUS is not on the headers. `3V3` is an output.
- **The DRV8313 needs VM ≥ 8 V.** That is why the USB path goes through the
  MT3608 boost module.
- **Wiring tables:** SimpleFOC Mini v1.0 and v1.1 have different header
  orders. Use `../datasheets/drv8313/README.md`.
- **Board underside:** the bottom of the board faces the drive's metal base
  and the spring contacts. Keep bottom-side parts and pours away from the
  spring pads (B.CrtYd of J2).
- **Measurement accuracy:** drive positions are from a rectified photo,
  ±0.5 mm. The fit-test print is the check, not the photo.
